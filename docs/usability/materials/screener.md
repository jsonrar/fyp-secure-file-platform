# Screener / 筛选问题

Ask these three before booking. Keep **no** personal data from anyone who is screened out.

预约前先问这三个问题。被筛掉的人，**不要**保留任何个人信息。

| # | Question | 问题 | Eligible answer 合格答案 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Do you study, work, or have you trained in IT, computer science or information security? | 你是否学习、从事或受过 IT、计算机或信息安全方面的训练？ | **No** 否 |
| 2 | Have you ever sent or shared a file online (email, a cloud drive such as Google Drive, a chat app)? | 你是否曾在网上发送或分享过文件（邮件、Google Drive 等网盘、聊天软件）？ | **Yes** 是 |
| 3 | Have you used or seen SecureView before? | 你以前使用或见过 SecureView 吗？ | **No** 否 |

If all three answers are eligible, record only: participant code (`P01`…), operating system,
browser, and the session time. Do not record a name against the data; keep the sign-up
sheet separate and destroy it after the sessions.

三个答案都合格时，只记录：参与者编号（`P01`…）、操作系统、浏览器和场次时间。不要把姓名与数据记在一起；
登记表单独保存，并在所有场次结束后销毁。
