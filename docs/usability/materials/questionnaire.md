# Post-test questionnaire / 测试后问卷

Give this on paper or a form after T10. The participant answers about **SecureView as they
just used it**. Record answers in `sus-template.csv` as 1–5.

在 T10 之后以纸质或表单形式发放。参与者针对**刚刚使用的 SecureView** 作答。把答案按 1–5 记入
`sus-template.csv`。

## Part A — System Usability Scale (SUS)

Scale / 量表: 1 = Strongly disagree 非常不同意 · 2 · 3 = Neutral 中立 · 4 · 5 = Strongly agree 非常同意

| # | Statement | 陈述 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | I think that I would like to use SecureView frequently. | 我想我会经常使用 SecureView。 |
| 2 | I found SecureView unnecessarily complex. | 我觉得 SecureView 不必要地复杂。 |
| 3 | I thought SecureView was easy to use. | 我觉得 SecureView 很容易使用。 |
| 4 | I think that I would need the support of a technical person to be able to use SecureView. | 我想我需要技术人员的帮助才能使用 SecureView。 |
| 5 | I found the various functions in SecureView were well integrated. | 我觉得 SecureView 的各项功能整合得很好。 |
| 6 | I thought there was too much inconsistency in SecureView. | 我觉得 SecureView 里有太多不一致的地方。 |
| 7 | I would imagine that most people would learn to use SecureView very quickly. | 我想大多数人会很快学会使用 SecureView。 |
| 8 | I found SecureView very cumbersome to use. | 我觉得 SecureView 用起来非常繁琐。 |
| 9 | I felt very confident using SecureView. | 我使用 SecureView 时很有信心。 |
| 10 | I needed to learn a lot of things before I could get going with SecureView. | 我需要先学很多东西才能上手 SecureView。 |

The SUS is by John Brooke (1996), "SUS: A quick and dirty usability scale". Wording is the
standard form with "the system" replaced by the product name. Items 4 and 10 are the ones most
tied to NFR2's "without any specialized knowledge".

## Part B — Open questions (answer in a few words)

1. What was the most confusing moment? / 最让你困惑的是哪一刻？
2. What, if anything, did you not understand the meaning of? / 有没有哪些词或提示你没弄懂？
3. Did you feel your files were safe? Why or why not? / 你觉得文件是安全的吗？为什么？
4. What would you change first? / 你会最先改什么？
5. Would you trust it with a real document? / 你会放心地把真实文件交给它吗？

Record answers verbatim in the notes column; they are the explanations behind the numbers.
