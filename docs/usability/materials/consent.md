## English

**What this is.** We are a Monash University final-year project team (FIT3162, team MCS21). We
built a web service, SecureView, for sharing files securely. We want to find out where it is
hard to use. **We are testing the service, not you.** Nothing you do can be wrong.

**What you will do.** About 55 minutes: try ten small tasks on the service while saying aloud
what you are thinking, then answer a short questionnaire. You will create a temporary account
using a passphrase you invent for today only. **Please do not use any password or passphrase
you use anywhere else.** We will never ask you to say it aloud. Your account and everything in
it is deleted at the end, as one of the tasks.

**What we record.** A participant code, your operating system and browser, how each task went,
how long it took, and what you say. **We do not record your name, email, or photograph.** Your
name appears only on a sign-up sheet that is destroyed after the sessions. [Recording: we will
NOT make audio or video recordings. / Edit this line if the team decides otherwise.]

**How it is used.** Results are summarised, without anything that identifies you, in our
project report and may be shown to our supervisor and assessors.

**Your choices.** Taking part is voluntary. You may skip any task or question, and stop at any
time without giving a reason and without any consequence. You can ask us to delete your data
up to the date we finish the analysis, which is __________.

**Questions.** Shuoxun Wen (testing lead), via the team's contact on the SecureView site, or the
